    >

- **Some people believe that protecting old forests is more important than building new homes. Others think that housing should come first. Discuss both these views and give your own opinion. Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.** _(KP `TR_W_OPINION`)_

  - Model answer:

    > In many growing regions, land that is covered by old forest could also be used for new housing. Some people argue that the forest must be protected, while others believe that homes for people are more urgent. In my view, both needs can be met if building is planned carefully.
    >
    > Those who want to protect old forests point out that they cannot be replaced. A forest that has grown for hundreds of years is home to many animals and plants, and it stores large amounts of carbon. Once it is cut down, a newly planted forest needs decades to offer the same benefits. Forests also give people places to walk and relax, which is important for health.
    >
    > On the other hand, people who support building argue that families need somewhere to live. In some cities, young people cannot afford rent, and new homes are the only way to lower prices. For them, a forest is less important than a safe and affordable home.
    >
    > I believe that the choice is often less extreme than it seems. Many cities have empty offices, old factories and car parks that could be turned into housing. Building on these sites first would provide homes without destroying old forests. Only when no other land is available should a small area of forest be used, and it should be replaced by new planting.
    >
    > In conclusion, both forests and homes matter, but careful planning means that we rarely have to sacrifice one for the other.

### TR-PS-WRITING (PRACTICE_SET) — Outdoor learning: opinion essay

**Section Opinion essay (WRITING)**

- **Some people think that children should spend more of their school day learning outdoors, for example in parks and gardens. To what extent do you agree or disagree? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience. Write at least 250 words.** _(KP `TR_W_OPINION`)_

  - Model answer:

    > Most children spend nearly all of their school day inside a classroom. I agree that they should spend more time learning outdoors, as long as outdoor lessons are planned as carefully as indoor ones.
    >
    > Firstly, many subjects are easier to understand outside. In a park, children can measure trees in a maths lesson, record bird species in science or describe the changing seasons in a writing class. Seeing real examples helps them remember what they learn far better than pictures in a textbook.
    >
    > Secondly, time outdoors is good for children's health. Fresh air and movement help them concentrate, and children who are often outside are less likely to become overweight. Outdoor lessons can also reduce stress, because a calm natural space feels very different from a crowded room.
    >
    > Some people worry that outdoor lessons waste time or are unsafe in bad weather. These concerns are reasonable, but they can be solved. Teachers can prepare clear tasks so that children know exactly what to do, and schools can provide waterproof clothing or move lessons inside during storms.
    >
    > In conclusion, I strongly believe that children benefit from learning outdoors. With good planning, more time in parks and gardens would make lessons more memorable and pupils healthier.
